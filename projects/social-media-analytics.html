Case Study

Social Media Analytics Dashboard

A dashboard concept that translates social media campaign data into actionable marketing insights across reach, engagement, traffic, conversions, and content performance.

Project Overview

Turning social media performance data into strategic campaign decisions.

This project demonstrates how campaign data can be organized into a dashboard that helps marketers understand what content is reaching the audience, what content is generating interaction, and which posts are contributing to traffic or conversion goals.

The dashboard is designed for practical decision-making. Instead of only presenting numbers, it connects performance patterns to clear marketing recommendations for future content, channel strategy, and creative testing.

Business Problem

Campaign teams often collect data but struggle to translate it into next-step decisions.

Social media platforms provide many metrics, but raw data can be difficult to interpret without a clear reporting structure. A post may receive strong impressions but low clicks, or high engagement but weak conversion. Without comparing metrics together, teams may repeat content that looks successful on the surface but does not support business goals.

  • Performance data is often spread across different platform reports.
  • Engagement metrics may be reviewed separately from traffic and conversion outcomes.
  • Top-performing content types are not always identified clearly enough for planning.
  • Marketing recommendations may rely on instinct instead of evidence from campaign data.

Dataset Description

A structured campaign dataset designed around common social media reporting needs.

The dataset represents post-level campaign performance across social media content types. Each row captures one published post or campaign asset, allowing performance to be compared by format, audience response, and funnel behavior.

Fields

Post date, platform, content type, campaign theme, and call to action.

Awareness

Impressions and reach show how widely campaign content was seen.

Engagement

Likes, comments, shares, saves, and engagement rate show audience interest.

Action

Clicks, click-through rate, conversions, and conversion rate show business movement.

Key Metrics

A balanced view of awareness, engagement, and conversion performance.

01

Impressions

Total content views, used to understand campaign visibility and message exposure.

02

Reach

Unique users who saw the content, used to evaluate audience coverage and brand awareness.

03

Engagement Rate

Audience interactions compared with reach or impressions, used to identify content relevance.

04

Click-Through Rate

The percentage of viewers who clicked, used to assess creative hooks and calls to action.

05

Conversion Rate

The percentage of clicks that completed a desired action, used to measure campaign effectiveness.

06

Top Content Types

Best-performing formats such as reels, carousels, stories, or static posts by campaign goal.

Dashboard Design

A clean reporting layout that supports fast interpretation.

The dashboard is organized around the marketing funnel so viewers can move from visibility to audience interaction to conversion outcomes. This structure helps a marketer quickly see whether a campaign problem is related to reach, content relevance, traffic generation, or conversion quality.

  • Top summary cards for impressions, reach, engagement rate, CTR, and conversion rate.
  • Trend chart showing performance changes over the campaign period.
  • Content type comparison to identify which formats drive the strongest outcomes.
  • Post-level table for reviewing individual high-performing or underperforming assets.
  • Recommendation area that translates metrics into marketing actions.

Insights Generated

Performance patterns can reveal what the audience values and where the campaign needs improvement.

Awareness

High impressions with lower reach may indicate repeated exposure to the same audience.

Engagement

High engagement rate can show which messages, visuals, or formats feel most relevant.

Conversion

Strong clicks with weak conversions may suggest landing page, offer, or audience-fit issues.

Marketing Recommendations

Use the dashboard to guide creative, channel, and testing decisions.

  • Increase investment in content types that combine strong engagement with strong click-through rate.
  • Revise hooks or calls to action when impressions are high but clicks are low.
  • Use high-save or high-share posts as signals for educational content and community value.
  • Test different landing page messages when clicks are strong but conversion rate is weak.
  • Build future content calendars around the formats and themes that perform best by campaign goal.

Tools Used

A practical analytics workflow for campaign reporting.

  • Spreadsheet-based dataset organization and metric calculations
  • Dashboard wireframe for reporting structure and visual hierarchy
  • Content type segmentation for comparing creative formats
  • Marketing funnel framework for insight interpretation
  • Recommendation checklist for turning data into action

Reflection

Good analytics work is not only about reporting numbers. It is about improving decisions.

This project helped me connect social media data with practical marketing strategy. Metrics such as impressions, reach, engagement rate, click-through rate, and conversion rate each answer a different question. The strongest insights come from reading them together rather than treating any single metric as the full story.

For graduate school applications and digital marketing internships, this case study reflects my interest in marketing analytics, consumer insights, and data-informed content strategy. It also shows how a dashboard can help teams move from campaign performance review to clearer recommendations for future creative decisions.
